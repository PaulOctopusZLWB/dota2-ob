# TI analytics contract set V1

This document is normative for the JSON shapes in `internal/contracts`. The Go
types and golden records are the executable source of truth. All records use
compact UTF-8 JSON, reject unknown fields on trusted ingestion, and identify
their exact schema with `schema_version`. A breaking field or semantic change
requires a new schema version; V1 fields are never repurposed.

`ObservedV1` is the common nullability primitive. Its state is one of
`present`, `absent`, `redacted`, `unsupported`, or `invalid`; `value` exists
only for `present`. This preserves provider-supplied zero and false values and
prevents missing data from becoming zero. Times are UTC RFC 3339 timestamps
when they describe source/storage time and integer milliseconds when they
describe deterministic policy time.

| Contract | Owner and provenance | Deterministic identity / clock | Bound | Forbidden content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `LiveObservationV1` | contract/live-projection; exactly one committed GSI `session.Record` and its raw payload | evidence `(session_id, sequence, raw_payload_sha256)`; GSI map/game clocks plus receive time; mapping version `gsi_normalized.v1` | 1 MiB, at most 10 participants | replay/history facts, account IDs, Steam IDs, player handles, credentials |
| `HistoricalSnapshotManifestV1` | history; sealed discovery, replay, parser, and aggregate inputs | content-addressed `snapshot_id`; UTC cutoff and maximum included event time | 1 MiB | active/incomplete matches, facts after cutoff, live observations |
| `HistoricalBaselineV1` | history; one immutable aggregate bound to a sealed snapshot | `baseline_id`, snapshot, roster/player/role/hero/patch/metric/window/sample key; UTC period and generation times | 1 MiB | current-match facts, silently mixed windows, unbounded samples |
| `InsightCandidateV1` | pure insight core; live observation plus optional sealed baseline | `candidate_id` derives from rule/config, ordered evidence, and snapshot; explicit policy milliseconds | 64 KiB | display prose, ambient wall-clock reads, adapter/storage state |
| `BroadcastDecisionV1` | pure policy core; candidate or operator command transition | `decision_id`, policy revision, causal candidate/command; explicit policy milliseconds | 32 KiB | localized prose, HTTP/auth/storage details |
| `OverlayStateV1` | presentation assembler; committed decision and localized catalog | decision and ordered evidence; source receive, publication, and stale times | 64 KiB; title 160 and body 1,024 characters | raw GSI, credentials, account IDs, replay-hidden facts, command URLs/privileges, arbitrary HTML, unbounded or invalid asset keys |
| `OperatorCommandV1` | authenticated delivery port; operator input only | unique command and bound session; expected revision and explicit policy milliseconds | 16 KiB; policy separately caps 4,096 unique IDs/session | direct state edits, credentials, display markup |
| `OperatorCommandResultV1` | pure policy result port | command/session plus previous and resulting revision | 32 KiB | transport/auth implementation details |
| `AuditEventV1` | pure policy audit output; always causal to a commit | event/session plus causal candidate/decision/command; explicit policy milliseconds | 16 KiB | independent persistence instructions, payload blobs |
| `PolicyCommitV1` | pure policy output; application supplies commit sequence | session and monotonic commit sequence, prior/resulting hashes and revisions | 256 KiB | filesystem framing, retry behavior, source envelopes |
| `PolicyCheckpointV1` | optional policy recovery cache | session/sequence/revision plus state and referenced-commit hashes; explicit policy milliseconds | 32 KiB | authoritative facts not present in its referenced commit |

`LiveObservationV1` deliberately duplicates no raw payload. Its participant
identity is the session-local `(team_key, session_slot)` and may additionally
carry a verified tournament identity. `internal/capture.MapLiveObservationV1`
is the only V1 mapping adapter. Existing raw, normalized analytics, events, and
diagnostic API shapes remain unchanged; their private identity fields stay in
the capture-owned legacy path and are unavailable to M1-M3 packages.

The dependency test permits only language-level deterministic helpers in the
contract and future pure insight roots. HTTP, filesystem, database, session,
analytics, replay/history, localization/presentation, delivery, GSI, capture,
and OBS adapter imports fail the build.
